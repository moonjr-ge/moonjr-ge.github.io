ZS-04 /LEPUS — 兔

///

GE://ZS-04_LEPUS:

Z-Series is a series of digital sculptures that reimagines the twelve animals of the Chinese zodiac as artificially modified human specimens. Animal characteristics are not treated as costumes or symbols, but as biological possibilities grafted onto the human body.

The series begins with the body as something unfinished. Rather than understanding the human form as a fixed biological condition, it imagines it as a mutable structure—one that can be extended, replaced, hybridized, and reconstructed. As the distinction between the natural and the artificial becomes increasingly unstable, bodily modification is no longer simply an alteration of the self, but a question of what the self is allowed to become.

Presented through a fictional classification system, the twelve sculptures are identified as individual experimental subjects according to their position in the zodiac cycle, transforming an ancient symbolic order into an imagined catalogue of post-human bodies.

These objects exist somewhere between organism and artifact. They are not representations of future humans, but speculative bodies through which the boundaries of the human can be reconsidered.

GE://ORGAN_KIOSK:

“ Welcome to ORGAN KIOSK!

We offer fully personalized custom orders to satisfy your every special need! Exceptional service, guaranteed quality — satisfaction guaranteed, peace of mind included! ”

Organ Kiosk is a spin-off of Organ Shop, developed as a casual management game set within an organ shop. Players run a small street-side organ kiosk, selecting, preparing, and selling organs according to the different needs of their customers, while keeping the shop running through a continuous cycle of transactions. Through the contrast between the lighthearted rhythm of a casual game and the trade of human organs, Organ Kiosk extends Organ Shop’s exploration of the commodification of the body, imagining a future in which it has become an almost ordinary part of everyday life.

GE://CRANE_MACHINE:

“Hello, welcome to Organ Shop! Good news：We are now offering a limited number of catch blind boxes! We have a wide range of products at affordable prices! Thank you for your custom and we wish you have a perfect event experience! ”

Crane Machine is a spin-off of Organ Shop, developed as a video game centered around organ blind boxes. Players operate a crane machine to randomly obtain blind boxes containing different organs, transforming body parts into entertainment commodities driven by chance, anticipation, and the desire to collect.

Crane Machine exists as an independent mini-game and will also become a playable element within Organ Kiosk, further expanding the virtual consumer world of Organ Shop.

ORGAN SHOP

///

GE://ORGAN_SHOP:

Organ Shop is a fictional online marketplace centered on body modification, where human organs are presented as ordinary commodities to be browsed, selected, and purchased.

By placing the body within the familiar language and logic of consumer culture, the work reimagines it not as something fixed or complete, but as a mutable system of interchangeable parts. Making bodily modification deliberately accessible, inexpensive, and mundane, Organ Shop blurs the boundaries between body, commodity, and desire, imagining a future in which the human form can be endlessly customized, replaced, and renewed.
